FARMER SHARE

The money is the product.

Through a mandi chain the farmer keeps 22–35% of your rupee (NABARD 2023). AgriLink flips the flow — fees are paid by the consumer on top, and the farmer's price is never touched.

🧮 The trust math

consumer pays = items + delivery + 8% platform fee − coupons − weekly basket − wallet
farmer gets = 100% of items — settled INSTANTLY to their UPI when you pay
rider gets = 100% of your tip
