# BhoomiDirect — Risk Register

Format: Likelihood / Impact on a High–Medium–Low scale, one named owner role each. Register version: MVP backend (Task 3-b).

## Top 3 (read this if you read nothing else)

1. **R3 — Instant-payout fraud & chargebacks** (High/High). Our differentiator — the farmer is paid the moment the consumer pays — is also our fraud magnet: a chargeback or failed collection after the farmer is already paid lands directly on the platform. RBI PA/PG authorization + per-farmer payout caps + device fingerprinting are gating items for real money.
2. **R2 — Fake farmer profiles / produce misrepresentation** (Medium/High). One viral "organic" scandal from an unverified seller kills the trust product. Aadhaar+liveness at onboarding, KVK badge, BhoomiChain field logs and spot audits are the four layers; three of them are currently mocked.
3. **R7 — Demand concentration on 5 pilot farmers** (High/Medium). The whole MVP runs on 5 people; one bad harvest, illness or churn breaks the storefront, and stock-outs kill the consumer habit we are trying to build. Waitlist + FPO aggregation must exist before any public launch.

## Review cadence

- Owner review: fortnightly during pilot, before each expansion gate.
- Risk triggers to re-open this register: first real-payout integration, first public consumer marketing, first DPDP audit, any security incident, monsoon/season transitions.
